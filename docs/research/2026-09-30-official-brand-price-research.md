# Official brand price research — 30 September 2026

## Purpose and decision rule

The owner's first-release target remains **all 63 active Shopify products (74 variants)**. The [63-product sheet](2026-09-30-official-brand-products.csv) groups each product's size and flavour choices on one row and links to one official brand page. The [74-variant detail sheet](2026-09-30-official-brand-price-research.csv) has one row for each active Shopify choice in the 30 September readback. For example, Applied Nutrition ISO-XP is one product page with 12 active TLL size/flavour combinations, not 12 separate brand listings. All 63 products now have a brand-site link, but the three Trained by JP links are to its official European store and the Mutant link is to its US store; those cannot establish a UK GBP direct price. A brand page or collection link alone also does not prove an exact pack. This is research for G1 product identity and price decisions; it is **not** approval to publish, make an item sellable, or change a Shopify price.

An **RRP** is a manufacturer-recommended retail price. A price at the manufacturer's own online shop is its current **direct selling price**, not an RRP unless the manufacturer expressly calls it an RRP. Tropicana's account-specific reply says it does not provide RRPs. Accordingly, no RRP is entered in the matrix. A family page may show a starting, selected, promotional or size-dependent price; it is not evidence of the exact TLL variant's price until the size and flavour are bound on the source page. All GBP observations are point-in-time and need rechecking before a pricing decision.

## Findings that affect the build

1. **No RRPs established.** Brand shop prices are comparison evidence only. Do not put them into Shopify `compare_at_price` or treat them as a price floor without separate approval.
2. **Pack identity remains the first check.** The current [Optimum Nutrition UK whey page](https://www.optimumnutrition.com/en-gb/products/gold-standard-100-whey-protein-powder-eu) lists 780 g among its options, while TLL has a 775 g listing. The exact current 775 g product needs confirmation before a brand price or formula is attached. The [Applied Nutrition casein page](https://appliednutrition.uk/products/micellar-casein-protein) calls the 900 g chocolate flavour “Chocolate Cream”, while TLL says “Chocolate”. These may be naming or product changes; do not silently equate them.
3. **The brand sites can help, but do not replace supplier cost.** Examples of direct GBP shop prices visible during research are [Darkstims Creatine 500 g £18](https://www.darkstims.com/products/darkstims-creatine), [Grenade Chocolate Chip Salted Caramel 12 × 60 g £22](https://www.grenade.com/products/protein-bar-chocolate-salted-caramel), and [Per4m Isolate Zero Chocolate Creme 900 g £49.99](https://per4mbetter.com/products/chocolate-creme-isolate-zero). These are not stated RRPs. Discounts, shipping and VAT treatment can change the commercial comparison.
4. **Placeholders need a chosen pack.** Thirteen active Shopify rows have £0 and `Default Title`. An official product-family page may identify the product, but no exact-size price can be assigned to an unspecified variant. These remain unavailable under the existing G1 hold.
