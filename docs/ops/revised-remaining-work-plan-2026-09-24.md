## Goal and observable completion

A customer can move from evidence-led comparison to an exact eligible Shopify variant and checkout in a coherent TLL experience, use one account and persistent basket/stack, receive accurate order and service information, and give or withhold marketing consent independently. Product and stock updates are reconciled; prices and promotions protect contribution margin; exceptions reach a human. Scores appear as scientific recommendations only after product/formula-specific approval. Operations can restore data, explain an order/price/stock decision, and stop a failed automation safely.

The public-launch gate requires: a proven hosted account/cart/order/logout journey; approved sellable catalogue and cost/shipping terms; coherent branded and accessible shop/comparison paths; lawful tested email capture and relevant service messages; working order/stock exception handling; backup/restore and release evidence. **Owner revision, 25 September:** neutral or clearly unassessed comparison products may remain visible at opening; all 229 scientific assessments are no longer a launch prerequisite. Unapproved numerical scores must not be presented as current assessments or recommendations. The initial shop may use approved manually controlled prices; automatic competitor pricing, flash sales and promoter affiliate discounts remain later full-project work.

## Material uncertainties and dependencies

1. **Stage 3 native Keychain failure — high coupling/blast radius, high evidence gap.** One synthetic cleanup journal is terminal `UNCERTAIN/CHILD`; file and sidecar remain unchanged. Its launcher collapsed several native outcomes. A read-only, metadata-only diagnostic and fixed secret-free categories must identify where it stopped before a new cleanup is designed. No replay or manual deletion. This is a safety gate for the current account/provider path, not a reason to rewrite working catalogue code.
2. **Hosted identity and commerce — high coupling/blast radius.** Staging Supabase provider, Vercel Preview, Shopify customer account/Storefront, session bridge and cart must be observed at one source revision. Dated provider and disabled-runtime reads do not prove the signed-in journey. Use the cheapest read-only state/route checks, then narrowly activate staging only after reviewed boundary evidence.
3. **Commercial source of truth — high ambiguity.** Direct TropShip account email says £5+VAT per order; generic public free-above-£100 text appears to concern wholesale accounts. Obtain supplier reconfirmation. Confirm wholesale VAT and pack identity, any surcharges, stock cadence, dispatch/returns, payment fees and the proposed customer £100-retail free-postage rule. One exact product/variant and one supplier-order scenario should be reconciled before catalogue-wide projections.
4. **Consent and email — high customer impact.** Shopify launch-list permission and comparison-site account email are separate. Recheck sender authentication and actual eligible automation timeline using an owner mailbox; no marketing send without separately reviewed audience and copy. Legacy subscribers need consent provenance.
5. **Research claims — containment is launch-critical, full coverage is later work.** Labels/formula versions and qualified category reviews are missing. Preserve neutral/unassessed public listings and suppress old numerical recommendations at opening. Pilot the complete evidence workflow on creatine, then scale across every product. Exact label facts for products TLL sells are a separate retail-information gate.
6. **Automation economics — high coupling.** Competitor observations, Shopify discounts, affiliate codes, refunds and supplier delivery interact. Start with read-only/shadow records and synthetic full-life-cycle accounting before any live price or code write.

The repeated failure pattern is an expensive one-shot credential window before its local failure modes were observable: consumed journals, Keychain ACL uncertainty, mismatched source/environment identity and coarse error reporting. The correction is to isolate native ports offline, record fixed non-secret categories, verify exact source/environment before arming, and run one bounded externally effective attempt only after a fresh baseline and independent review. A new generation number alone is not a fix.
