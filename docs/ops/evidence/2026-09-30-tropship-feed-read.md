Without a supplier observation timestamp and update explanation, its stock is not a current availability approval.

**Complete-file validation failed.** Python's strict XML parser stopped with `junk after document element: line 140915, column 11`; the separate `xmllint` parser also rejected line 140915 as extra content after the document. The source closes `Products` after a complete `Product` record and then continues with product fields and more records. This is a structural error in the downloaded bytes. The 14 September CSV parser's complete-accounting rules cannot be assumed to validate this XML. The source must be corrected or replaced and then parsed from start to finish before any automated stock or price decision.

A bounded diagnostic read of the *complete prefix only* counted 8,807 product records before that structural error. Against the 74 active Shopify SKUs in the [G1 register](2026-09-30-g1-variant-dispositions.csv), the readable prefix contains 60 SKU codes and lacks 14. All 13 active £0 placeholders are among the 14 absent codes. Twenty-four of the 60 matched codes occur twice in the prefix; each pair agrees on the checked stock, price, tax, size, barcode, name, brand and flavour fields. These are diagnostic counts, not proof that the complete file has no later records or that duplicate rows can safely be collapsed. The remaining old-feed-linked SKU may occur after the break, be absent from the file, or have changed identity; the partial read cannot decide which.

**G1/G2 result: HOLD.** The feed is malformed, its advertised freshness is unproven, its duplicates need a documented source meaning, and the supplier's account-specific VAT, pack and stock semantics remain unconfirmed. Do not import or price from the readable prefix. Request a corrected, complete feed and the supplier's written meaning for duplicate rows and update time. Once received, reconcile all 74 active variants against exact pack and label evidence, then apply the existing commercial and stock gates.
