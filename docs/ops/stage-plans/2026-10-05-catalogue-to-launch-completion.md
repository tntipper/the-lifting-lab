## Price record design to implement in Step 3

For each exact variant, record the comparison product ID, Shopify product/variant ID, supplier SKU, verified barcode/pack/flavour/formula mapping; UK manufacturer domain and option-specific URL; GBP regular brand price where evidenced, current brand price and promotion type; tax-inclusive customer-price basis; observed-at time, review/version/expiry and retained source evidence. Keep explicit manufacturer RRP separate and nullable. Store observations without overwriting history. Baseline refresh can be daily (proposed 24-hour validity), while supplier stock reads follow the confirmed hourly cadence; stale observations block new automated price decisions.

Store private wholesale costs and approval records in protected server-only tables; publish only the minimal approved product/price/availability view. Test table grants and row access in the isolated database. Do not overwrite the present retail_price blindly or assume the source column identifies price provenance. Apply a bounded reviewed import by exact IDs with before/after counts and readback, with price writes still disabled until approved.

Suggested public wording is “Brand UK price” with a dated source where useful. Do not turn a third party's price into TLL's own “was” price. RRP, savings and strikethrough claims need genuine supporting price evidence; see [ASA reference-price guidance](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/recommended-retail-prices-rrp.html).

## Delivery, returns and VAT details

Recommended decision to review: **£6.99 standard customer delivery per eligible single TropShip fulfilment group**, with no free-delivery threshold initially. It covers the confirmed £6 supplier charge and leaves £0.99 before payment fees/other costs; it is a proposal, not a configured tariff or guaranteed profit. Prove that a customer basket maps to the charged supplier order(s); do not multiply £6 by item quantity or silently undercharge multiple supplier orders. Unsupported postcodes/services require documented coverage or an honest unavailable checkout state.

Translate supplier terms into TLL customer terms, rather than copying them unchanged. The supplier permits sealed unwanted returns within 14 days and a £6 QR-code return service or own-cost return. Faults/missing items go to Customer Service; cancellation after warehouse processing and failed deliveries require exception handling. The email does not define every fee/remedy. TLL must preserve applicable customer cancellation/refund rights, including the standard-delivery refund; do not automatically deduct the supplier return fee from every customer refund. [GOV.UK returns guidance](https://www.gov.uk/accepting-returns-and-giving-refunds) and [price-transparency guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/price-transparency-cma209/providing-clear-and-accurate-information-about-prices-summary) inform the customer policy and basket display.

Record the non-VAT-registered calculation now; make a later VAT-registration transition explicit rather than silently changing historical prices. Monitor taxable turnover on a rolling 12-month basis and the separate expected-next-30-days test. [GOV.UK registration guidance](https://www.gov.uk/register-for-vat).
