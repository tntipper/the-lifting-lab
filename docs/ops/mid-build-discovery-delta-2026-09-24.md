## Business decisions and external evidence still needed

- **First public opening boundary — DECISION NEEDED:** May the shop open with clearly labelled unassessed products and no effectiveness rankings while research review continues, or must all categories be approved first? The owner has asked for every category to be verified, while the original plan also permits honest incremental publication. This changes what blocks opening. Owner answer pending.
- **Automation at first opening — DECISION NEEDED:** Must competitor repricing and affiliate-specific offers be operational before opening, or may the initial shop use approved manually controlled prices while those later controls are completed? Full project scope includes both. Owner answer pending.
- **Customer-facing commerce policy — DECISION NEEDED / EXTERNAL DEPENDENCY:** Confirm customer delivery charges/free-shipping rule, dispatch wording, cancellation, returns and support promises against Tropicana's actual commercial terms. Supplier delivery cost is not the customer's delivery charge.
- **Affiliate programme terms — DECISION NEEDED:** Confirm who may join, eligible products/orders, attribution window, code stacking, discount/commission rates, returns window, payout approval and budget. Until then, no code or payout should be treated as commercially authorised.
- **Marketing eligibility — EXTERNAL EVIDENCE:** Recheck Shopify sender authentication and test headers; inspect actual checkout reminder conditions and suppression; review legacy consent evidence. Use current-form confirmed subscribers for offers and keep account/service messages separate.
- **Research evidence — EXTERNAL DEPENDENCY:** Obtain manufacturer labels/formula revisions and qualified outcome-specific review for each product/category. Unknown products remain unassessed, without inherited scores or safety endorsements.

## Operational steps the product alone cannot remove

The owner or named reviewer must approve ambiguous pack mappings and costs, supplier and customer policies, campaign economics and scientific claims; monitor exceptions and returns; and approve any customer marketing send. Tropicana, Shopify, payment and email providers supply behaviour that local code cannot establish by itself. A no-purchase test cannot prove live fulfilment.

**DISCOVERY READY FOR DEV AUTOPILOT.** The product intent is clear enough for technical reassessment. The two launch-boundary answers and the listed external commercial/evidence inputs remain explicit gates; technical reconnaissance must not turn them into assumed approvals.
