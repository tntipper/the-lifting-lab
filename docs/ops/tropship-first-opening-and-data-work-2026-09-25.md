# TropShip-first shop and product-data work

Owner direction, 25 September 2026: the first shop range should favour TropShip-supplied products. For comparison products outside that range, use a reviewed outbound retailer affiliate link where an exact offer exists. Consider buying wholesale stock for high-demand brands after trading data shows demand. Build the customer journey and a safe, smaller opening range before optional commercial automation. No supplier order, public offer or live shop setting changed by this document.

## What changes in the customer journey

The comparison site should present one clear next action for each product: **Buy from The Lifting Lab** only for an exact, approved, in-stock TropShip sellable variant; **View at partner retailer** for an approved, exact outbound affiliate listing; or **No verified offer** when neither is confirmed. An outbound affiliate purchase happens on the partner's site and cannot share the TLL cart, checkout, account or fulfilment. The existing link code already distinguishes an exact external listing from retailer search results and shows affiliate disclosure; it does not verify the current price or pack. Do not turn a search result or merely matching product name into a purchase promise. Existing TLL promoter codes/commissions are a **different** affiliate programme and remain later work.

The 25 September read-only Shopify export contains 98 variants, of which 74 SKUs occur in the 14 September supplier feed and 61 are active in Shopify. Shopify writes product status only on the first variant row; counts must carry that status across the same product handle or the active range is undercounted. The [74-SKU research queue](../research/data/tropship-first-evidence-queue-2026-09-25.csv) is an investigation list, **not** an approved opening range. At least 13 active £0 placeholders and 12 ISO-XP zero-stock/continue-selling variants need a non-purchase visibility and inventory check before promotion. Products may be left out of the first range without blocking the site flow.

## Two data streams, kept separate

**Supplier operations and costs.** TropShip `ProductCode`, barcode, size, price, tax flag and stock feed are useful input candidates. The 14 September file has a non-zero wholesale price for each of the 74 matched variants, with 71 `VAT` and three `Zero` flags. Twenty have no nutrition text. The file has **no RRP** or description field. Shopify `Variant Compare At Price` and `Cost per item` are empty for all 98 variants, so the current Shopify price must not be relabelled as manufacturer RRP. The old supplier stock values cannot prove availability today. The account email supports a £6 gross supplier delivery allowance **per submitted TropShip order**, but supplier VAT basis, current costs and exact chargeable pack still require written confirmation.

**Manufacturer facts for customer pages.** A bounded independent source pilot checked ten varied SKUs. Manufacturer pages were often product-family pages without a barcode-to-label match. More seriously, the old supplier nutrition text conflicts with manufacturer information for Strawberry ISO-XP (`APP511`) and Barebells Cookies & Cream (`BAR007`); the Grenade row `GRE091` names another flavour in its nutrition text; the Strom SupportMAX capsule row `STM044` includes powder-flavour ingredients. See the source links and individual findings in the [research queue](../research/data/tropship-first-evidence-queue-2026-09-25.csv). The supplier nutrition field must be treated as a research lead, **never automatically copied to a customer product page**. Shopify already has product-body text, but sampled descriptions are generic and refer to comparison scores that are currently withheld; those bodies must be reviewed against exact labels and the current score-display rule before they are used for a promoted opening range.

Research workers can collect candidates in parallel, but publication needs an exact brand/product/flavour/size/pack identity match plus a manufacturer UK/EU label, specification, official product page or manufacturer confirmation. Barcode/GTIN or manufacturer part number should tie the source to the sellable variant; for a carton, verify both the individual unit and case quantity. Record source URL, retrieval date, version or content hash, ingredients, allergens, nutrition per serving/100g, directions, warnings and any claims. A reviewer resolves disagreement and image-only labels. Write original short descriptions from verified product type, flavour, size and factual features. Do not copy marketing text or add unverified efficacy, certification or health claims. The existing score/claim publication guard remains in force.

## Price and RRP checks

Use the **actual proposed TLL selling price**, not RRP alone, to test margin. For a one-item TropShip order, start with customer price after discounts, subtract the VAT-inclusive wholesale cost actually charged, £6 supplier delivery, payment fee, a returns allowance and any promoter discount/commission. The existing disabled calculator enforces a **35% target, 25% minimum and £3 minimum cash contribution per item**; it also tests whole baskets and each item separately. The £6 is per supplier order, not per item; the standalone item check conservatively assumes its own order. A manufacturer RRP, once sourced for the exact UK pack and date, is a market reference and must be stored separately from TLL's chosen price. It cannot establish profit without the actual selling price and full cost.

The earlier [current-price screen](evidence/2026-09-25-current-shopify-variant-and-offer-addendum.md) warns that 55 of 64 simple feed-linked non-multipacks would miss the item safeguards under the proposed 10% customer discount plus 10% first-order commission even on favourable assumptions. Keep that promotion and customer free-postage offer disabled for opening unless a specific reviewed basket passes. If the supplier cannot provide RRP, that alone need **not** stop a manually approved price or a functioning shop flow. A current, correctly taxed landed cost and exact sellable pack **are** needed before a SKU is sold at that price.

## Ordered work with small proofs

1. **Customer flow first.** Continue the existing Stage 3 staging account/cart/order/logout acceptance at its documented HOLD gate; prove the comparison-to-exact-variant path and honest external-link fallback without a live purchase. This does not depend on obtaining RRPs for all products. Preserve the current disabled production controls.
2. **Candidate opening range.** Start with the 61 active TropShip-feed-linked variants; two of those are explicit multipacks (ten across the full 74-SKU queue) and need separate case-pack pricing. Reduce to a small set whose exact pack, current stock path, current costs and customer-facing facts can be proved. Mark the rest HOLD or outbound-only. Fix the active £0 placeholders and zero-stock overselling before public promotion. Check one SKU end to end before scaling.
3. **Research and content pipeline.** Divide the 74-SKU queue into non-overlapping batches. Collect manufacturer sources and new wholesale-feed snapshots; compare exact labels and units; write original draft descriptions; review conflicts independently; only then publish approved fields. A changed label, barcode, flavour, case pack or supplier price reopens review for that field. The pilot's four conflicting SKUs stay HOLD.
4. **Commercial check.** Obtain Tropicana's written reply to the [draft email](drafts/tropship-account-confirmation-2026-09-25.md), the actual payment tariff and a reasonable returns allowance. Run RRP-versus-cost as a market screen where genuine RRP exists; run actual proposed TLL price through the existing item and basket calculator. Approve a small range manually. Automate feed reads and proposed changes in **shadow mode** (reports only) before any price/stock writes.
5. **Later growth.** Use trading data to identify high-demand gaps and consider wholesale stock only after storage, cash, VAT, delivery and returns economics are known. Promoter affiliate codes, flash sales, competitor repricing, paid campaigns and automatic price publication remain later, separately proved work.

**Launch distinction:** Missing RRP, broad competitor data and full automation can be parked if a smaller manually reviewed range and a truthful affiliate fallback work. Missing exact pack identity, accurate displayed ingredients/allergens, current landed cost, stock/fulfilment controls, customer terms and a tested checkout path cannot be parked for a product that TLL sells. The owner has now approved keeping comparison products visible as neutral or clearly unassessed while product-by-product scientific review continues. This supersedes the earlier all-229-assessments-before-opening rule. Unassessed products must not carry an effectiveness recommendation, rank or unapproved numeric score that could imply scientific approval.
